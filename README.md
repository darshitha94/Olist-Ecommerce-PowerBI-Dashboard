🚀 Olist E-Commerce Analytics Dashboard
Project Overview
This project analyzes the Olist Brazilian E-Commerce dataset to understand sales performance, customer satisfaction, and delivery operations.

The project was developed using Excel, SQL, Power BI, and DAX.

💡 Key Business Insights
São Paulo generated the highest revenue among all states.
Delivery success rate exceeded 97%.
Average customer review score remained above 4 out of 5.
Credit Card was the most preferred payment method.
Delivery delays significantly reduced customer satisfaction.
Beauty & Health was one of the top-performing product categories.

✅ Project Outcome
The analysis showed strong sales performance, high customer satisfaction, and efficient delivery operations.

The most important finding was that delivery performance has a direct impact on customer review scores and overall customer experience.
